## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the high-velocity, precision-engineered world of real-time on-device artificial intelligence. Rooted in an aesthetic of hyper-efficient computational authority, the interface serves engineers, creators, and power users navigating intensive neural workflows on next-generation Snapdragon Copilot+ hardware. 

The visual language combines glassmorphism with technical minimalism.

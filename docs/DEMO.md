# CogniEdge Hackathon Demonstration Guide

Step-by-step walkthrough to demonstrate the complete 3-layer architecture during judging.

---

## 1. Demo Narrative Arc

1. **Launch CogniEdge Desktop**: Open the desktop app (`npm run dev` or Electron build).
2. **Hardware Detection Verification**:
   - Point out the active detected hardware in the top header and Dashboard (GPU, CPU, NPU).
   - Explain honest provenance: no fake metrics or hardcoded marketing claims.
3. **Start Game Session**:
   - Click **Start Session** on `/dashboard` or navigate to `/live`.
   - Show the live YOLO screen-vision state (HP, Shields, Ammo, Minimap threats).
4. **Push-To-Talk Voice Query**:
   - In `/live`, submit a tactical voice query: *"Why am I losing duels in East Corridor?"*
   - Show Qwen3-4B synthesizing game vitals with cross-session player memory.
5. **Trigger AI Performance Doctor**:
   - Navigate to `/performance`.
   - Observe the live frame-time consistency graph with stutter anomaly markers.
   - Show the deterministic bottleneck diagnosis (*VRAM Pressure / Asset Streaming Risk*).
   - Show the predictive stutter risk score (*82% probability in next 1.5s*).
6. **Adaptive AI Compute Guard (Safe Optimization)**:
   - Review proposed action: *Reduce AI Vision Sampling 3 FPS → 1 FPS*.
   - Click **Preview** then **Apply Optimization**.
   - Show frame variance stabilizing and 1% low recovery.
7. **End Session & Post-Game Coaching**:
   - Click **End Game Session**.
   - Navigate to `/gaming-coaching` to see the generated debrief and cross-domain correlation (deaths coinciding with texture streaming spikes).
8. **Player Memory & Advice Effectiveness**:
   - Navigate to `/memory` to show recurring player habits and verified advice ROI (81% success rate).
9. **A/B Benchmark Verification**:
   - Navigate to `/benchmark` to run the measured AI preset comparison against traditional GPU overlays.
